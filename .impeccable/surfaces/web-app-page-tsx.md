# Landing

Mode: Persuade. A first-time visitor from a shared link must understand within seconds what this is, believe the picks are reasoned, and enter a team ID.

Audience and job: FPL managers, casual to stats-curious. Action: enter a team ID or paste a Points page link. Proof: a labelled sample board drawing a move; one move with its stat line ("every pick explained"); the league stake (gap, edge, differentials); real backtest numbers from /api/model-check, never invented. Then how to find your ID, and a not-affiliated footer.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The landing is the tactics board before the team talk: the board is already up with a sample squad and the first move drawn, and the team ID box is the chalk-free invitation to put your own counters on it. It refuses the hero-headline-plus-feature-cards template.

OWN-WORLD: Same world as the app: white melamine board, aluminium frame, blue and red magnet counters, yellow armband, marker annotations, condensed grotesque for data. On this Persuade surface the board can own the full first viewport and marker annotations can speak at larger scale.

STORY: Visitor sees a real-looking board with a move drawn and its gain, reads one line on what the app does, enters their team ID; scrolling shows how one pick is explained, how the league view works, and how accurate the model has been.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: product name; one-line promise; team ID input with submit directly under it; the sample board below, labelled Sample, a move arrow drawn with its gain. Desktop: promise and input left, board right at large scale. Primary action: the team ID submit.

FORM: Dressing-Room Tactics Board, IMPECCABLE'S PICK (position 1; roll assigned position 4). seed:5a2ea8218325360b.
